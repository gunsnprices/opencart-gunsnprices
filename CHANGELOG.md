# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-11

First release.

- Cost per round of ammunition on product pages and in product lists (category, search, specials, brands, related
  products, Featured, Latest, Bestsellers and Specials modules). Uses the displayed price: special price, tax setting
  and the shopper's currency.
- Round count from the product name, a product attribute or a product option (cost per round for every option value).
- Firearm specs (caliber, magazine capacity, barrel length) in the Specification tab or at the top of the description.
- Per-store settings, "Try a product name" box, optional caliber links to GunsNPrices.com (off by default).
- Builds for OpenCart 4.0.2.3 and 4.1.0.x (`gunsnprices_cpr.ocmod.zip`) and OpenCart 3.0.4.x/3.0.5.x
  (`gunsnprices_cpr_oc3.ocmod.zip`). Events only, no core file changes, no OCMOD.
- Bundles the gunsnprices PHP parser 0.1.0 (MIT, data CC BY 4.0).
